McRecorder

Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last updated: 10 September 2026

McRecorder is a voice recorder with on-device speech-to-text, published by iDataGear Inc. It is available for iPhone on the App Store and for Android on Google Play.

The short version: your recordings never leave your device. We do not collect them, we cannot read them, and there is no account or server to send them to. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.

Part 1 — Privacy Policy

What we collect

Nothing. McRecorder has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. We do not collect, transmit, store or have access to any personal information about you. We do not operate a backend server for this app and receive no data from it.

In App Store terms, this means no data is collected and no data is used to track you. In Google Play Data safety terms, it means no data collected and no data shared.

What stays on your device

The following live in the app's own private storage and are never uploaded anywhere:

WhatWhere
Your audio recordings (16 kHz mono WAV) The app's private Documents/recordings folder
Transcripts produced by speech-to-text A .txt file beside each recording
Waveform caches A .peaks file beside each recording
Your settings (interface language, theme, storage limit, auto-save interval, skip-silence) The platform's own app-preferences store
The downloaded speech model The app's private application-support folder

Uninstalling the app removes all of it. Deleting a recording inside the app removes its audio, its transcript and its waveform cache together.

Speech-to-text runs on your device

Transcription is performed entirely on your device by a speech model that runs locally. Your audio is not sent to us or to any third party for transcription — not while recording, not afterwards, and not for the live text shown while you record.

The one network request the app makes

The app connects to the internet for exactly one purpose: downloading the speech model, once, the first time you ask for text. The model is about 228 MB and is fetched from one of:

  • https://github.com — a pinned release asset
  • https://huggingface.co — a public copy of the same model, used if the first source fails

This is an ordinary file download. No audio, no transcripts, no identifiers and no personal information are sent with it. After the download completes, transcription works with the device offline.

Those hosts will see the network request itself, including your IP address, as they would for any file download. Their handling of that is governed by their own privacy policies.

When you choose to share

The app can share a recording's audio or its transcript through the platform's share sheet, and can copy a transcript to your clipboard. This only ever happens when you tap Share or Copy. What happens to the content afterwards is governed by whichever app you send it to. We are not involved and receive nothing.

Permissions, and why each one exists

PermissionPlatformWhy
Microphone iOS & Android To record audio. This is the app's purpose. It is requested the first time you tap record.
Internet Android Only to download the speech model, as described above.
Background audio iOS So a recording continues when you leave the app or lock the screen. iOS displays its own recording indicator for the whole time.
Foreground service, microphone type Android So a recording continues when you leave the app or lock the screen. Android requires a visible, persistent notification for the whole time this runs.
Notifications Android To show that recording notification. Recording still works if you deny it; you just do not get the notification.
Wake lock Android Held only while recording, so a device going to sleep does not cut your audio off.

The app requests no location, camera, contacts, or shared-storage permissions, and does not read files outside its own folder.

Purchases

McRecorder has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions on either store. There is nothing to buy, nothing to restore, and no payment information is ever requested or handled by the app.

Data sharing and sale

We do not share or sell any personal data. There are no third-party advertising, analytics or attribution partners in the app.

Data retention and deletion

All app data lives on your device, for as long as you keep it there. We hold none of it, so we retain nothing. You can delete any recording from within the app, or remove everything at once by uninstalling the app.

Children

McRecorder is a general-purpose utility. It is not directed at children under 13, and because it collects no data it collects no data from children either.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, export, correct or delete. Your recordings are yours, on your device, under your control: rename them, share them, delete them, or uninstall the app to remove everything at once.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above changes with it. Because the app collects nothing, any change would be a clarification rather than a new use of your data.
